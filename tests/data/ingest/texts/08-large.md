# Composting

A plan for the compost bays behind the shed.

## Bays

Three bays side by side: one filling, one cooking, one finished. Each is a cubic metre of pallets
lined with mesh.

## What goes in

Greens: kitchen scraps, grass clippings, spent plants. Browns: dry leaves, shredded cardboard,
straw. Aim for roughly two parts brown to one part green by volume.

## Turning

Turn the cooking bay every two weeks. It is ready when it smells of earth and nothing in it is
recognizable — about three months in summer.
